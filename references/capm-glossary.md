# CAPM Glossary

Quick-reference glossary of key CAPM terms, organized alphabetically.

## A
- **Acceptance Criteria**: Conditions a deliverable must satisfy to be accepted
- **Activity**: A distinct, scheduled portion of work performed during a project
- **Actual Cost (AC)**: Realized cost incurred for work performed on an activity
- **Agile**: Iterative approach to project management/software development
- **Assumption**: A factor considered true, real, or certain without proof

## B
- **Backlog**: Prioritized list of work to be done (Agile)
- **Baseline**: Approved version of a work product used for comparison
- **Benefit-Cost Ratio (BCR)**: Ratio comparing benefits to costs of a project
- **Budget at Completion (BAC)**: Total planned value of the project

## C
- **Change Control Board (CCB)**: Group responsible for reviewing/approving changes
- **Charter**: Document authorizing the project and PM authority
- **Constraint**: A limiting factor affecting project execution
- **Contingency Reserve**: Budget/time allocated for identified risks
- **Critical Path**: Longest sequence of activities determining shortest project duration
- **CPI (Cost Performance Index)**: EV/AC ratio measuring cost efficiency

## D
- **Deliverable**: Any unique, verifiable product/result produced to complete a process
- **Dependency**: Logical relationship between activities
- **Definition of Done (DoD)**: Criteria that must be met for work to be considered complete

## E
- **Earned Value (EV)**: Value of work actually completed
- **Enterprise Environmental Factors (EEFs)**: Conditions not under project team control that influence the project
- **Estimate at Completion (EAC)**: Expected total cost of the project

## F
- **Fast Tracking**: Schedule compression by doing activities in parallel
- **Float (Slack)**: Time an activity can be delayed without impacting the project end date
- **Functional Organization**: Structure where staff report to functional managers

## G
- **Gantt Chart**: Bar chart showing project schedule visually

## I
- **Increment**: Sum of completed product backlog items (Agile)
- **Integrated Change Control**: Process of reviewing, approving, managing changes
- **Iteration**: Time-boxed development cycle (Agile)

## K
- **Kanban Board**: Visual tool showing workflow status
- **Kickoff Meeting**: Meeting to launch project/phase, align stakeholders

## L
- **Lag**: Delay inserted between activities
- **Lead**: Acceleration applied between activities (overlap)
- **Lessons Learned**: Knowledge gained during the project for future use

## M
- **Milestone**: Significant point/event in the project (zero duration)
- **MVP (Minimum Viable Product)**: Simplest version of a product with core features

## N
- **Net Present Value (NPV)**: Present value of future cash flows minus initial investment

## O
- **OPAs (Organizational Process Assets)**: Plans, processes, policies used and updated by the org

## P
- **Planned Value (PV)**: Budgeted cost for scheduled work
- **Portfolio**: Collection of projects/programs managed together
- **Precedence Diagram**: Network diagram showing activity relationships
- **Product Backlog**: Prioritized list of desired work/features (Scrum)
- **Product Owner**: Role responsible for maximizing product value (Scrum)
- **Program**: Group of related projects managed in a coordinated way
- **Progressive Elaboration**: Iteratively increasing detail in a plan

## Q
- **Quality Assurance (QA)**: Process-focused, proactive quality activities
- **Quality Control (QC)**: Product-focused, inspecting deliverables

## R
- **RACI Matrix**: Responsible, Accountable, Consulted, Informed - roles chart
- **Requirement**: A condition/capability needed by a stakeholder
- **Reserve**: Time or budget set aside for risk
- **Retrospective**: Team reflection meeting to improve process (Agile)
- **Risk**: Uncertain event/condition that may affect project objectives
- **Risk Register**: Document listing identified risks and responses
- **ROI (Return on Investment)**: Measure of investment profitability

## S
- **Schedule Performance Index (SPI)**: EV/PV ratio measuring schedule efficiency
- **Scope Creep**: Uncontrolled expansion of scope without adjustments
- **Scrum Master**: Servant leader who facilitates Scrum process
- **Sprint**: Time-boxed iteration in Scrum (1-4 weeks)
- **Sprint Backlog**: Items selected for current sprint
- **Sprint Retrospective**: Meeting to reflect and improve
- **Sprint Review**: Meeting to demo completed work
- **Stakeholder**: Anyone affected by or who can affect the project
- **Story Points**: Relative unit of effort estimation (Agile)

## T
- **Triple Constraint**: Scope, Time, Cost (sometimes extended with Quality, Risk, Resources)
- **Trend Analysis**: Examining project performance over time

## U
- **User Story**: Short description of a feature from the user's perspective

## V
- **Value**: The worth, importance, or usefulness of something
- **Velocity**: Amount of work a team completes per iteration (Agile)
- **Variance**: Difference between planned and actual performance

## W
- **WBS (Work Breakdown Structure)**: Hierarchical decomposition of project scope
- **Work Package**: Lowest level of the WBS

---

**Note**: Original glossary compiled for CAPM exam preparation reference.
